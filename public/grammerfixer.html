Frequently asked questions

How does the AI Grammar Fixer work?
It uses a large language model to analyze your text in full context and correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and sentence structure. Unlike rule-based tools, it understands meaning — so corrections sound natural, not robotic.
Is this grammar checker completely free?
Yes, completely free. No account, subscription, or payment is required — open the page and start fixing your grammar instantly.
What types of grammar errors does it fix?
Subject-verb agreement, verb tenses, article usage (a/an/the), spelling, punctuation, run-on sentences, sentence fragments, word order, and awkward phrasing — covering virtually all common English grammar mistakes.
Can I use this for essays and professional writing?
Absolutely. It works well for essays, emails, cover letters, reports, and business documents. The AI preserves your original meaning and voice while improving grammatical correctness and clarity.
How is this better than Grammarly or traditional checkers?
Traditional checkers apply fixed grammatical rules and often flag correct sentences as wrong or miss context-dependent errors. This tool uses a language model that reads the full sentence for meaning, making corrections that are both accurate and natural-sounding. And it is free with no paywall.
