Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this AI language translator?
It is powered by large language models that understand context, idioms, and nuance — significantly more accurate than rule-based or older statistical systems. It handles complex sentence structure and cultural context naturally.
Which languages does this AI translator support?
16 languages: English, Bangla, Arabic, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Korean, Turkish, Vietnamese, and Western Punjabi.
Is this AI translator free to use?
Yes, completely free. No account, sign-up, or payment is required. Just open the page, type your text, and translate.
How do I translate English to Bangla?
Select English as the source language and Bangla as the target language. Type or paste your text into the left box, then press Translate. You will get a natural, accurate Bangla translation in seconds.
Can I download my translation as a PDF?
Yes. After translating, press Download PDF. A formatted PDF containing both the original text and the translation will be saved to your device.
